aaSpeed Foreground Service Demo

This video demonstrates aaSpeed's use of FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION for real-time GPS speed tracking while riding/driving, including motorcycle pocket use.

For Google Play Console declaration - shows: open app, grant location, start tracking, persistent notification, background / screen off continuation, stop tracking.

Note: Google Play requires a YouTube unlisted link for the foreground service declaration form (it validates youtube.com format). This GitHub Pages hosted MP4 is for preview/reference only. Upload this same MP4 to YouTube as unlisted and use that youtube.com/watch?v= link in Play Console.
