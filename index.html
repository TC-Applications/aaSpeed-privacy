Privacy Policy for aaSpeed

Effective: 30 August 2026 — App: aaSpeed (com.tcapps.aaspeed) & aaSpeed Pro (com.tcapps.aaspeedpro)

Summary: aaSpeed does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data off your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Location Data

  • What: Precise GPS location to calculate speed (mph / km/h) and GPS accuracy (±m).
  • How: Google Play Services FusedLocationProvider with high accuracy.
  • Where processed: Entirely on-device. No server.
  • Storage: Speed held in memory only while foreground service runs. No history saved to disk.
  • Transmission: Location never leaves your phone. No upload, no sharing.
  • Foreground service: Keeps GPS alive when phone in pocket / screen off for Android Auto. Notification: "Tracking speed for Android Auto".
  • Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, POST_NOTIFICATIONS.

No Data Collection

No account, no name, email, or identifiers. No analytics or ad SDKs.

No Data Sharing

We do not sell or share data. There is no data to share.

User Control

Revoke location anytime in Android Settings > Apps > aaSpeed > Permissions. Stop tracking by closing app or dismissing notification. Unit preference (mph/km/h) stored locally in SharedPreferences.

Children

Not directed to children under 13.
